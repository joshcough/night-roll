## Texture & arrangement

- **Inverted texture** — the accompaniment role and melody role swap
  voices: the "melody" line supports while another voice (often bass)
  carries the tune. — `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj, whole
  A part (Josh's thesis: "the bass is the real melody"). FF: *(find one —
  MM2 imports are a promising hunt)*.
- **Texture hierarchy** — one voice leads, the others make room; busy +
  busy = soup. — `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj (still
  pedal melody over driving bass). FF: *(find one)*.
- **Motivic saturation** — the signature idea appears in every layer;
  at cadences it reads as arrival, not redundancy. — `encountered`
  2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj close — add9 in chords, melody, then
  deliberately propagated into the bass (C♯3). FF: *(find one)*.
- **Breakdown / dropout preparation** — removing an established layer
  reads as a stumble unless the exit is prepared: phrase boundary,
  marked edge (crash/fill), section-length duration. — `encountered`
  2026-08-18, learned by direct experiment (the failed bar-6.3 drum
  cut in Cool Bmaj). FF: *(find one — where does the corpus thin its
  texture, and how is the edge marked?)*.
- **Ostinato** — a short pattern repeated obstinately; the steady layer
  other voices float against. — `encountered` 2026-08-17. Anchor: Cool
  Bmaj bass gallop. FF: *(find one)*.

## Rhythm

- **Cross-rhythm** — two simultaneous layers dividing time differently
  (triplets over straight); works when one layer holds the pulse
  steady. — `encountered` 2026-08-17, used twice before naming. Anchor:
  Cool Bmaj — triplet turn over straight bass; straight-16th hats under
  the triplet melody. FF: *(find one)*.
- **Complementary rhythm** — voices offset so one moves where the other
  holds, filling each other's gaps. — `encountered` 2026-08-17. Anchor:
  Cool Bmaj, rhythm guitar vs bass ("moving in very slightly different
  rhythms... it just works"). FF: *(find one)*.
- **Agogic accent** — emphasis by DURATION: the long note is the heavy
  note, no volume needed. — `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj
  — snares placed on the bass's long notes. FF: *(find one)*.

## Melody & motive

- **Pedal tone** — a note held (or restruck) while harmony moves under
  or over it. — `encountered` earlier sessions; used deliberately.
  Anchor: Cool Bmaj — B5 through the chord sway, then the F♯6 a fifth
  up. FF: *(find one)*.
- **Varied repetition** — repeats earn a change; identical passes go
  stale ("it did the same exact thing every time"). — `encountered`
  2026-08-18, applied twice unprompted (melody F♯6 lift; the groove
  iteration). Anchor: Cool Bmaj fourth pass. FF: *(find one)*.
- **Augmentation** — a motive restated in longer note values (its slow-
  motion self). — `encountered` 2026-08-17. Anchor: Cool Bmaj — the
  bass walkdown is the melody's E–D♯–B turn, augmented. FF: *(find
  one)*.
- **Contour** (and contour reversal) — a line's up/down shape,
  independent of exact pitches; reversing it refreshes a repeat. —
  `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj final walkdown — descent
  becomes rise-leap-settle via one note. FF: *(find one)*.

## Harmony

- **add9** — a triad plus the 9th (2nd), color without the 7th. —
  **`demonstrated`** 2026-08-17: Josh named the closing Badd9 BY EAR
  before any analysis, then deliberately propagated the 9 into the
  bass. Anchor: Cool Bmaj close (D♯–F♯–C♯ over ringing B; bass C♯3).
- **Tendency resolution (9→3)** — color tones lean somewhere; the 9
  resolving up to the 3 (C♯→D♯ in B) is why the spice lands instead of
  hanging. — `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj final bass
  notes. FF: *(find one)*.
- **Lament bass / Andalusian descent** — bass falling stepwise (or
  chromatically) from tonic toward the dominant; grief's bassline. —
  `encountered` 2026-08-17 (progression library: Grieving). Anchor:
  *(none in Josh's songs yet)*. FF: *(find one)*.

## Form

- **Turnaround** — the moment before a repeat that sets up the RETURN
  (vs. a B part, which departs). Often the C part in game loops. —
  `encountered` 2026-08-18. Anchor: Cool Bmaj — the final four bass
  notes before the loop jump, differentiated from the bar-4 seam.
  Also Josh's own Overworld label "C — borrowed + turnaround"
  (2026-08 analysis). Study thread lives in open-items.md.
- **Intro + loop (game form)** — heard-once material, then a body that
  cycles; the seam placement is a composition decision. — arguably
  **`demonstrated`** 2026-08-18: Cool Bmaj's loop lands where the drums
  enter (2.4), built unprompted. FF: every capture in the corpus.
